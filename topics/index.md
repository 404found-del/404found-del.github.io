Every essay on this site belongs to one of six running threads. Pick the one
closest to your problem.

- **[Modeling](/topics/modeling/)** — facts, dimensions, keys, and history: shaping data for analysis.
- **[Architecture](/topics/architecture/)** — warehouses, lakes, layers, and stacks: the structural decisions.
- **[Governance](/topics/governance/)** — ownership, contracts, lineage, and meaning: what makes data trustworthy.
- **[Engineering](/topics/engineering/)** — pipelines, CDC, batch and streaming: moving data safely.
- **[AI](/topics/ai/)** — what generative AI changes about data architecture, and what it demands of it.
- **[Craft](/topics/craft/)** — the practice and the role of the data architect itself.
