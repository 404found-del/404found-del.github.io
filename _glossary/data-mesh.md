**Data mesh** is an organisational approach to analytical data that
decentralizes ownership: instead of one central team running one platform,
domain teams (orders, payments, logistics) own and publish their data as
**products**, with a self-serve platform underneath and *federated* governance
holding standards together.

Its four principles — domain ownership, data as a product, self-serve platform,
federated computational governance — are a response to central-team bottlenecks
in large organisations. The honest track record: the sociology is the hard part.
Mesh succeeds where domains genuinely staff and own their data products (usually
formalised through [data contracts](/glossary/data-contract/)), and fails where
it's adopted as a technology purchase. Small organisations rarely need it — a
bottleneck you don't have doesn't need decentralising.
